# PPSC / FPSC English Lecturer (BS-17) & Subject Specialist Exam Prep Portal

An interactive web preparation portal and offline study suite for the **Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC)** and **Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC)** English Lecturer (BS-17) & Subject Specialist examinations.

---

## 🏛️ Official PPSC Exam Blueprint & Criteria

- **Total Questions**: 100 MCQs
- **Duration**: 90 Minutes
- **Negative Marking**: **-0.25 Marks** per incorrect response (1.0 for correct, 0.0 for unattempted)
- **Minimum Qualifying Score**: **40.0 Marks (40%)**
- **Test Syllabus Composition**:
  * **80% Subject Specialization**:
    - Classical & Romantic Poetry (Chaucer, Milton, Donne, Pope, Wordsworth, Keats, Shelley, Byron)
    - Drama & Theatre (Shakespeare, Marlowe, Webster, Shaw, Ibsen, Beckett, Miller, Pinter)
    - Novels, Fiction & Prose (Bacon, Swift, Austen, Brontë sisters, Dickens, Eliot, Hardy, Woolf, Joyce, Orwell)
    - Modern & Post-Modern Poetry (Yeats, Eliot, Larkin, Plath, Frost, Hughes, Heaney)
    - Linguistics & Phonetics (Phonology, Morphology, Syntax, Semantics, Pragmatics, Sociolinguistics, Chomsky, Saussure)
    - Literary Theory & Criticism (Aristotle, Longinus, Sidney, Dryden, Arnold, Eliot, Structuralism, Post-Colonialism, Feminism)
    - American & World Literature (Achebe, Soyinka, Rushdie, Roy, Hemingway, Faulkner)
    - History of English Literature & Literary Eras
    - English Grammar, Vocabulary & Figures of Speech
  * **20% General Ability Quota**:
    - Pakistan Affairs, Islamic Studies, Everyday Science, Math, Current Affairs

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **2,240+ Solved & Categorized MCQs Bank**:
   - Comprehensive harvesting across English literature, linguistics, criticism, and general knowledge.
   - Integrated solved past papers from 2011 to 2024 (PPSC 2011, 2015, 2017, 2020, 2022; SPSC 2023; KPPSC 2021).
2. **PPSC 100-MCQ 90-Minute Mock Exam Simulator**:
   - Full question palette navigation (Answered, Flagged, Visited, Blank).
   - Real-time countdown timer with 5-minute warnings.
   - PPSC negative marking engine calculating: Correct (+1.0), Incorrect (-0.25), Net Score, and Pass/Fail (≥ 40 threshold).
   - "Retake Missed Questions" button for focused revision.
3. **Subject-Wise Practice Modules**:
   - Practice by individual literary genre or linguistic domain.
   - Toggle between **Learn Mode** (instant explanation & answers) and **Quiz Mode**.
4. **MCQ Explorer & Repository**:
   - Instant search across all 2,240+ MCQs with topic filters and pagination.
5. **Progress Log & Analytics**:
   - Interactive Chart.js graph tracking your net score progress across attempts.
   - Saved bookmarks for quick reference on challenging authors and concepts.
6. **Master PDF Guide (Two-Column Self-Study Book)**:
   - 2,240+ questions formatted with questions on the left and verified answers on the right for zero-spoiler practice.

## 🚀 Local Setup & Running

```bash
# 1. Clone repository
git clone https://github.com/iasad12/ppscenglishprep.git
cd ppscenglishprep

# 2. Install dependencies
pip install -r requirements.txt

# 3. Run application
python app.py
```
Open [http://127.0.0.1:5001](http://127.0.0.1:5001) in your browser.
